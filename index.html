About Maggie Paterson

Founder, UK Barre Academy

Maggie Paterson is the Founder of UK Barre Academy and brings over 40 years of experience in ballet, dance, Pilates, yoga and fitness education.

Following advanced ballet training and the achievement of her British Ballet Organization (BBO) Teaching Diploma in Ballet and Tap, Maggie enjoyed a professional career as a dancer and choreographer before establishing herself as a highly respected teacher and educator.

Throughout her career, she has taught at some of the UK’s most recognised dance and fitness institutions, including Pineapple Dance Studios, London Studio Centre, Danceworks, Pilates Off The Square and David Lloyd Clubs. Her teaching experience spans ballet, jazz, Pilates, yoga, barre fitness and movement conditioning, working with everyone from complete beginners to aspiring professionals.

Maggie’s extensive background in both dance and fitness has enabled her to develop a unique teaching methodology that bridges the gap between classical ballet training and contemporary exercise. Drawing upon decades of experience, she has created the UK Barre Academy method to provide a safe, effective and accessible approach to ballet-inspired fitness for today’s instructors and clients.

The UK Barre Academy Philosophy

The UK Barre Academy method combines the technical foundations of classical ballet with the core strength principles of Pilates, the mobility and mindfulness of yoga, and the accessibility of modern fitness training.

The result is a structured, ballet-informed barre programme that prioritises alignment, posture, movement quality and safe progression while delivering a challenging and enjoyable full-body workout.

Maggie’s mission is to train instructors to teach barre with confidence, precision and integrity, preserving the artistry of ballet while making its benefits accessible to a wider audience.
