Module 01

Introduction to UK Barre Academy

By the end of this module, learners will understand the philosophy, origins and benefits behind the method.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the UK Barre Academy philosophy.
  • Understand the origins of ballet barre training.
  • Recognise the benefits of ballet-inspired fitness.

Topics Covered

  • History of Ballet
  • Evolution of Barre Fitness
  • The UK Barre Academy Method
  • Benefits of Ballet-Inspired Movement

Module 02

Ballet Fundamentals

By the end of this module, learners will demonstrate correct posture and apply core ballet terminology.

Learning Objectives

  • Demonstrate correct ballet posture.
  • Understand alignment principles.
  • Apply basic ballet terminology.

Topics Covered

  • Neutral Alignment
  • Turnout Principles
  • Positions of the Feet
  • Positions of the Arms
  • Core Engagement
  • Ballet Terminology

Key Teaching Points

  • Lengthen through the spine
  • Engage deep abdominals
  • Maintain shoulder stability
  • Work within natural turnout

Module 03

Warm-Up Principles

Preparing the body safely for movement through mobility and activation.

Class Duration · 5 Minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Prepare the body safely for movement.
  • Understand mobility and activation.

Topics Covered

  • Joint Mobilisation
  • Dynamic Stretching
  • Activation Drills
  • Movement Preparation

Module 04

Pilates Core Conditioning

Building trunk stability through classical Pilates principles.

Class Duration · 8 Minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Develop trunk stability.
  • Understand Pilates principles.

Topics Covered

  • Neutral Spine
  • Breathing
  • Pelvic Stability
  • Core Activation

Exercises

  • Roll Down
  • Pelvic Curl
  • Toe Taps
  • Single Leg Stretch

Module 05

Classical Barre Technique

The technical core of the method — five classical exercises, taught with precision.

Class Duration · 15 Minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Demonstrate classical barre exercises safely.
  • Apply correct ballet terminology.

Topics Covered

  • Pliés
  • Relevés
  • Tendus
  • Développés
  • Battements
01 Pliés
  • Knees track over toes
  • Maintain posture
  • Control movement
02 Relevés
  • Weight through first and second toes
  • Lift through the arches
03 Tendus
  • Fully articulate the foot
  • Lengthen through the working leg
04 Développés
  • Control range of motion
  • Maintain core stability
05 Battements
  • Precision before height
  • Stable standing leg

Module 06

Barre Fitness Conditioning

Layering fitness elements onto the technique, safely and with purpose.

Class Duration · 8 Minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Incorporate fitness elements safely.

Topics Covered

  • Pulse Work
  • Isometric Holds
  • Arm Weight Sequences
  • Muscular Endurance

Module 07

Stretch and Mobility

Improving flexibility safely, with an understanding of recovery technique.

Class Duration · 5 Minutes

Learning Objectives

  • Improve flexibility safely.
  • Understand recovery techniques.

Topics Covered

  • Hamstring Stretch
  • Hip Flexor Stretch
  • Adductor Stretch
  • Spinal Mobility
  • Breathwork

Module 08

Teaching Skills

Moving from performing the method to delivering it, professionally.

Learning Objectives

  • Deliver a professional barre class.

Topics Covered

  • Verbal Cueing
  • Visual Demonstration
  • Hands-On Corrections
  • Progressions
  • Regressions
  • Class Management

Practice Teaching

  • Learners teach a short section of the format.

Module 09

Assessment

The final checkpoint before certification — practical and written.

Practical Assessment

Learners must demonstrate:

  • Safe technique
  • Correct terminology
  • Effective cueing
  • Professional delivery

Written Assessment

 

  • Ballet terminology
  • Alignment principles
  • Class structure
  • Safety considerations

The Format

UK Barre Academy class structure.

Every module feeds into a single, structured 50-minute class — the same shape, every time.

  • 01Warm-Up5 mins
  • 02Pilates Core8 mins
  • 03Lower Body Preparation4 mins
  • 04Pliés5 mins
  • 05Cardio & Coordination5 mins
  • 06Arm Weights5 mins
  • 07Développés & Battements5 mins
  • 08Stretch & Mobility8 mins
  • 09Cool Down5 mins
Total Class Time 50 Minutes
